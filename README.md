#Calorie and budget tracker
Domain
Data Analysis & Visualization.
APp collects, processes and analyzes nutritional and financial data.

There are many calorie-tracking apps and websites. However, none of them combine calorie tracking with a budget tracker that allows users to check the cost per calorie and per gram of carbohydrates, protein and other macros. My goal is to integrate both, making it easy to identify the best way to fulfill nutritional requirements according to any budget.

The application first collects data on the user's current and desired weight, workout plan and budget. Then, it provides projected daily calorie and macronutrient requirements and expected day-to-day nutritional intake.After that, users can enter information about their daily meals and their prices. The application calculates the cost per calorie and per gram of each macronutrient, as well as total daily, weekly and monthly food expenses.
